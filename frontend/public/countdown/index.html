What features are included in the Hub? expand_more

Smart assignment tracking with deadline countdowns, automated submission alerts, lab extension and administrative request ticket management, syllabus & previous question paper repositories, and department announcements.

TSEC Assignment Hub is a paid, on-demand academic service. You simply submit your assignment, practical journal, or project requirements, pay an affordable student-friendly price, and get complete, 100% accurate, professor-ready work delivered directly to you before your deadline!

The first 25 students who register their email on this waitlist before our official launch on 5 October at 7:00 PM will automatically receive 5 pages of assignment or journal work completely FREE on their first order with zero upfront payment!
