Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services and security.

How does the registration and verification work?

expand_more
You register with your basic details and email address. A secure 6-digit verification code is instantly emailed to you. Once verified, your student profile is created and linked directly to your campus!

Is my student identity kept confidential?

expand_more
Yes, 100%. We take privacy extremely seriously. All student data is protected with database-level Row Level Security (RLS) policies. Your identity, email, and files are never shared with anyone.

What happens if I need changes or revisions?

expand_more
Every assignment solution includes free revisions within the scope of your original requirements to ensure total alignment with your professor's instructions.

Can I log in without entering an OTP every time?

expand_more
Yes! The OTP verification is only a one-time step during initial account creation. Subsequent logins only require your registered email and password.
